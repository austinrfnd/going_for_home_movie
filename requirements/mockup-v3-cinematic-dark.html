In the aftermath of the Altadena fires, a Little League community confronts loss and discovers what home truly means.

When the Eaton Fire swept through Altadena in January 2025, it destroyed not just homes and schools, but the baseball fields where this community had gathered for generations. The fire took everything visible—but it couldn't take what was invisible.

Going for Home follows the Altadena Little League through their first season after the disaster. It's a story of loss, yes, but also of what remains when everything is stripped away: the bonds between neighbors, the rituals that give life meaning, and the stubborn human need to rebuild.
