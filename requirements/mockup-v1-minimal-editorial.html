A Documentary Film

Going for Home

The Altadena Little League community rebuilds after the devastating fires
Directed by [Director Name] 2025 · Documentary · 40 min
Watch on Vimeo Criterion Channel

In January 2025, the Eaton Fire tore through Altadena, California, destroying homes, schools, and the beloved Little League fields that had been the heart of this tight-knit community for generations. Going for Home follows the families, coaches, and young players as they navigate loss, displacement, and the uncertain path toward rebuilding.

Through intimate access and unflinching observation, the film captures moments of grief and resilience, documenting how a community bound by America's pastime finds strength in each other while confronting questions about home, belonging, and what it means to play on.
