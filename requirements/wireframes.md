## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Palette (Based on Reference)
- **Primary Accent**: Green (logo/title treatment)
- **Background**: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Text**: Black (#000000)
- **Dividers**: Light gray (#CCCCCC)

### Typography
- **Logo/Title**: Custom or Sans-serif display font
- **Headings**: Sans-serif, bold weight
- **Body Text**: Sans-serif, regular weight
- **Awards/Credits**: Sans-serif, bold for categories

### Layout Principles
1. Minimalist, text-forward design
2. Generous white space
3. Horizontal dividers between sections
4. Content centered with max-width container
5. Single column layout (simple, elegant)

### Interactive Elements
- Clickable hero image (links to trailer/Vimeo)
- Navigation links with hover states
- Social media icons
- Email links
- Ticket/info buttons on screenings
