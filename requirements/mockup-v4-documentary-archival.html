Going for Home

The Altadena Little League community rebuilds after the fires

I.

The Story

When the Eaton Fire swept through Altadena, California in January 2025, it destroyed homes, schools, and the beloved Little League fields that had been the heart of this tight-knit community for generations. For the families who had gathered there every spring for decades, the loss was immeasurable.

Going for Home follows the community through their first season after the fires. With intimate access to families, coaches, and young players, the film documents the quiet acts of resilience that emerge in the aftermath of disaster—the way neighbors show up for each other, the rituals that persist, and the stubborn human need to rebuild.

Through the lens of America's pastime, the documentary examines larger questions about home, belonging, and community. What makes a place home? What do we carry with us when everything else is gone? And how do we teach our children to face loss while holding onto hope?
