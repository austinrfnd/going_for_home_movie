A Documentary Film

Going for Home

In the aftermath of the Altadena fires, a Little League community discovers what it truly means to come home.
Director[Director Name]
Runtime40 minutes
Year2025
Watch on Vimeo Criterion Channel PBS

When the Eaton Fire swept through Altadena in January 2025, it took homes, schools, and the baseball fields where generations of families had gathered. But it couldn't take the bonds that held this community together.

Going for Home follows the Altadena Little League through their first season after the fires—a story of children learning resilience, parents finding strength in each other, and a community discovering that home is something you carry with you.
